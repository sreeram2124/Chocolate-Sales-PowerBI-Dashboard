An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze chocolate sales performance, profitability, shipment trends, product performance, geographic distribution, and salesperson performance.

## Key Metrics

- Total Sales: $64M
- Total Profit: $37M
- Profit Margin: 57.2%
- Total Boxes Sold: 4M
- Shipment Count: 11K

## Dashboard Features

- Current Year vs Previous Year sales comparison
- Current Year vs Previous Year boxes sold comparison
- Shipment distribution analysis
- Geographic sales analysis
- Top-performing products
- Product-level profitability analysis
- Top salesperson performance
- Interactive date filtering
- KPI cards for key business metrics

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## Business Insights

The dashboard enables users to identify:

- High-performing products
- Products generating losses or low margins
- Geographic markets contributing the most revenue
- Top-performing salespeople
- Shipment volume patterns
- Year-over-year sales trends
- Overall business profitability
